MISSION BRIEF

Abstract

During combat engagement, a vehicle-mounted remote-controlled weapon system (RCWS) must maintain precise line-of-sight (LOS) tracking despite vehicle-induced disturbances. Roll, pitch, yaw and heave motions of the vehicle can significantly degrade turret pointing accuracy. This work presents a multi-rate cascaded architecture for precision LOS tracking under realistic vehicle kinematics and sensor non-idealities. The architecture combines a 1 kHz outer position loop, a 10 kHz kinematic feedforward compensator and a 20 kHz inner Sliding Mode Control-Active Disturbance Rejection Control (SMC-ADRC) loop with an Extended State Observer.
