An RF-Energy-Harvesting Architecture for Smart Waste Monitoring

Chhavi Singh · 2025

← BACK TO PROJECT ARCHIVE

MISSION BRIEF

Abstract

Battery-powered sensing nodes can simplify waste-bin monitoring, but fleet-wide battery replacement creates recurring maintenance and material costs. This paper presents a design concept for a battery-free waste-monitoring node that combines ambient radio-frequency (RF) energy harvesting, threshold-controlled energy storage, fill-level sensing, and event-driven IEEE 802.15.4 telemetry. A mains-powered gateway associates received reports with a known collection area and forwards them to a cloud service for dispatch planning. We formulate a first-order energy-balance model and calculate idealized recharge intervals for a 5-mJ sensing-and-transmission event over a range of assumed net harvested powers.
